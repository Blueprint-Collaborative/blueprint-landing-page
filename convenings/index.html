The shape

Before, during, and after.

Most of what decides whether capital moves happens on either side of the event itself. We work the whole arc.

Leading up

We gather the needs

We work directly with community stewards to surface and vet what they need, turn it into fundable opportunities, and share it through the channels already reaching your people, in your voice. No new tool, no new channel, nothing new to learn.

During

Deepen in the room

Attendees arrive already familiar with the work, so the time together goes straight to the conversations that move it. Blueprint and our partners sit alongside the stewards for a practitioner session built around what the cohort actually needs.

After

Carry it forward

Momentum turns into capital deployed into the work, with peer co-funding visible to the people who move together. The pipeline stays live after everyone flies home.

What we add

Needs that arrive grantable and investable.

01 — Readiness

Ready to fund, not just to read

Every opportunity arrives with structure, diligence, and legal infrastructure already in place, so someone moved by a steward's work can act on it that week.

02 — Instruments

Across all four instruments

Grants, recoverable grants, loans, and equity coordinated from one place, so stewards raise the mix their work actually needs instead of pitching each instrument separately.

03 — Co-investment

Several funders, one need

We actively convene the capital. A single need can draw from several funders at once, and from funders outside your community, so no steward depends on one person saying yes.

04 — Data

Built on data that already exists

We start with what stewards already have and build from there, working with them directly. Nothing new is asked of your team, and no reporting burden lands on top of the work.

05 — Placement

Where your community already gathers

We embed into the platform, roster, and channels your people already use. We review what you run before we propose anything. You stay at the center throughout.

The point

You built the room

Getting the right people into one place at the same time is the hardest part, and you have already done it. This is the infrastructure that turns that room into deployed capital.

How we start

A founding cohort, chosen with you.

We start with a small cohort of community stewards and the funders positioned to back them, selected together with your team.

Deliberately small, so every steward's need gets real attention. Large enough that the room has both sides of the table in it.

The first cycle is also the proof. What works becomes the pattern you run at scale in the years after.

We are approaching capacity on what we can take on, and we are deliberate about where we go deep. The window before a convening is a runway, and runways have a length.

Surfacing and structuring needs is a months-long process. A conversation that starts close to the date becomes a conversation about the following year.

  • 01 A gathering people already attend We work with the convening power you have already built.

What makes it work

It works best when four things are true.

The pre-work is what makes a room convert, and it is substantial. So we are specific about the conditions that make it pay off.

Where these are in place, a convening reliably moves capital. Where they are missing, we will say so early and point you toward something that fits better.

Condition 01

Capital in the room

The results come from rooms where people who can deploy are sitting with the people doing the work.

Condition 02

Your name on the send

Opportunities go out under your name, through your channels. That is where the reach comes from, and it is why your community pays attention.

Condition 03

Stewards who want in

Surfacing a real need is close collaborative work with the organization that holds it. The stewards who lean in produce the opportunities that get funded.

Condition 04

A real after

Deployment shows up in the months following the event, so the value compounds where someone on your side stays with it.
